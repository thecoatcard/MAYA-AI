# Third-party notices (backend)

## Lucide icons

The slide icons in `app/automation/deck_icons.py` are hand-converted from, or drawn in the style
of, the [Lucide](https://lucide.dev) icon set (24 x 24 grid, 2 px round-capped strokes) and are
written as native PowerPoint freeform shapes.

Lucide is licensed under the ISC licence; parts of it derive from Feather icons (MIT licence,

<https://github.com/lucide-icons/lucide/blob/main/LICENSE>.
